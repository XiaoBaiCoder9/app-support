Privacy Policy

This policy describes how our apps handle information. It applies to every app published by , unless a separate policy is shown inside that app.

Per-App Practices

Select an app to review its specific data practices. The general policy below applies to all apps.

General Policy

  1. Information we process

    Our apps process the content you choose to create or import — such as scanned documents, recognized text, images, signatures, notes and settings. We do not require you to create an account to use the core features.

  2. On-device processing

    Where a feature runs locally, your content is processed and stored on your own device. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your documents for advertising or profiling.

  3. Optional cloud and sync storage

    Some apps offer an optional sync feature through your own private cloud account (for example, Apple iCloud / CloudKit). When you enable it, your data is stored under your account and is accessible only to you. We do not operate a separate developer server for your app content, except where an app description states otherwise.

  4. Purchases and subscriptions

    In-app purchases, subscriptions and restore actions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive transaction information needed to unlock purchased features, but we do not receive your credit card, billing address or full payment details. Subscription terms, pricing and renewal rules are shown on the in-app purchase screen.

  5. Advertising, analytics and tracking

    Our apps do not show ads and do not use advertising identifiers. We do not track your activity across other apps or websites, and we do not share personal data with third-party advertisers. Any embedded third-party SDK, if present, is listed in the per-app practices above and processes data only as described by its own provider.

  6. Data retention and deletion

    Content stored on your device remains there until you delete it inside the app or remove the app from your device. Deleting the app removes its local data. If sync is enabled, removing synced content follows the rules of the cloud provider you chose.

  7. Children's privacy

    Our apps are designed for the age rating shown on each app's store page. Where an app is directed to children, it is built to collect no personal data, with no account, advertising, analytics or third-party tracking; any learning progress stays on the device. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us so we can remove it.

  8. Security

    We follow reasonable practices to protect information handled by the apps. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit access to app data to what the device and the platform provide.

  9. Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy as apps change or as required by law. The “effective date” at the top reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be noted on this page and, where appropriate, inside the app.

  10. Contact

    If you have questions, requests or concerns about this policy or how an app handles your information, reach us through the contact below. When writing, please specify the app name and version.
